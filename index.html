MADE FOR THE WAY YOU TRAVEL

One thoughtful place
for every next stop.

From the first curious search to the final shared postcard, Wayfarer keeps the whole journey connected. Explore the capabilities below to see how each one works.

⌕01 / DISCOVER

Find a place that feels like you.

Explore destinations by mood, region, pace, and the kind of moments you want to collect.

Explore this feature ＋
Search by destination or travel mood, then narrow the collection with curated categories such as coast, culture, nature, food, and slow travel. Each result includes a quick sense of the place, a suggested trip length, and ideas to help you decide whether it belongs on your map. Your signed-in searches appear in History; guest searches stay in the current session only.
⌖02 / PLAN

Turn a thought into a journey.

Shape a practical day-by-day outline around your dates, travel party, budget, and interests.

Explore this feature ＋
The planner uses your destination, trip length, party size, budget range, and selected interests to shape a structured itinerary. It groups ideas into morning, afternoon, and evening, estimates a trip budget, and creates a packing checklist. You can revise the inputs and generate another version. It is a planning assistant, not a live booking engine—verify opening times, transport, and prices before travel.
◷03 / HISTORY

Your travel thinking, remembered.

Return to the destinations you explored and the plans you have already shaped.

Explore this feature ＋
When signed in, Wayfarer saves your destination searches and generated plans to your private account history. Reopen a plan, inspect its original details, or use it as a starting point for a new route. Guest mode does not save personal history; guest activity is deliberately not written to your account or server.
↗04 / SHARE

Make a trip worth sharing.

Create a picture card from your plan and send the idea to the people coming along.

Explore this feature ＋
Choose a generated itinerary and turn its destination, trip dates, route highlights, and travel mood into a designed image card. Download it as a PNG, use your device's share sheet when supported, or copy a shareable link containing the itinerary details. A recipient can open the link to preview the shared trip without needing your account.
✳05 / GUIDE

A guide that shows you around.

Take a step-by-step tour that moves through the actual workspace and explains what matters.

Explore this feature ＋
The interactive tour navigates to each main area, highlights the relevant control or content region, and explains the purpose, inputs, and expected result. Move forward or backward at your own pace, or stop the tour whenever you like. The guide is available from the landing page and inside the dashboard.
◎06 / ACCESS

Travel first. Sign in when ready.

Use the complete discovery, planning, and sharing experience as a guest.

Explore this feature ＋
Guest mode is not a locked demo. You can use destination discovery, build itineraries, calculate estimates, and generate share cards. The difference is privacy and continuity: guest history is not saved. Sign in with Google or email to keep your searches and plans attached to your account across sessions.
