THE WORKSPACE, EXPLAINED

More than a content checker.
A complete control layer.

Every feature has a defined job, a clear boundary and a place in the workflow. Open a feature to understand what it does, when to use it and how it protects your team.

▤01 / REVIEW

Source-aware review queue

Submit web copy, campaign drafts, product announcements or social content. Keep every review, finding and decision together.

Explore this feature ＋

The review queue is the operational centre for content checks. A draft keeps its title, channel, original text, submitter, findings, comments and current status. Agent 37 can compare the draft with approved material and identify mismatches, unsupported claims or missing context. Findings include explanations and suggested next steps so reviewers can understand what needs attention rather than receiving a score without reasoning.

Reviewers can request changes, place an item on hold or record approval. A creator cannot approve their own submission, preserving separation between writing and sign-off. BASE records the outcome so the team can see how a draft reached its final state.

◈02 / KNOWLEDGE

Approved brand sources

Build one trusted library for messaging rules, product facts, terminology and policies.

Explore this feature ＋

Store the material your organisation has already approved: brand guidelines, product claims, legal wording, product facts, tone-of-voice rules and preferred terminology. Give each source a clear name and type, add the approved text and optionally set a review date. This keeps the evidence base organised and easier to maintain.

Analysis is limited to sources in the active workspace. Agent 37 should cite the source behind a finding or clearly state that the concern is unverified. Source management is restricted to authorised brand owners and administrators.

✳03 / ASSISTANT

Agent 37 workspace assistant

Ask questions, navigate the product and prepare actions with a confirmation step.

Explore this feature ＋

Agent 37 is designed as an assistant for the BASE workspace. It can explain the product, help locate a feature and prepare supported operations such as creating a review, adding a source or updating a record. A proposed change is presented with its intended effect and waits for your confirmation before execution.

Guest mode offers a guided preview and general product help. Live workspace actions require an authenticated account and a deployed, configured Agent 37 API. The assistant does not independently approve or publish content.

⌁04 / ANALYTICS

Operational insights

Understand recurring issues, severity patterns and how reviews move through the workflow.

Explore this feature ＋

Insights aggregate findings saved with review records. Teams can see which categories appear repeatedly, how many high-severity findings remain and how many submissions have been approved. Use those patterns to clarify a policy, improve a source document or update a content process.

Numbers in a signed-in workspace come from its actual saved records. The guest demo labels its example information as demonstration data rather than presenting it as your organisation’s results.

♧05 / COLLABORATION

Team roles & workspaces

Keep teams, responsibilities and projects organised around clear access boundaries.

Explore this feature ＋

Workspace roles separate day-to-day creation from source management and review decisions. Administrators manage access, brand owners maintain approved knowledge, reviewers handle decisions and creators submit content. Each workspace keeps its own sources, reviews and members, so one project’s information is not blended with another.

Membership and data access are enforced by Supabase row-level security policies in the connected application. Guest mode shows an example team and does not invite real people.

◷06 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Audit history

Follow submissions, source updates, comments and decisions in one activity timeline.

Explore this feature ＋

Audit history organises recorded workspace events by time, action and actor. It helps answer practical questions: who submitted this draft, which source changed, when was a decision made and what happened next? Use the timeline alongside review details when you need to understand the path a piece of content followed.

Production compliance programmes should pair application events with trusted server-side or database audit controls. The interface does not claim that a client-generated event log alone is tamper-proof.

Review queue

Submit drafts, compare them with approved guidance and keep decisions traceable.

How the review queue works +

Each review stores the original draft, its current status, source-linked findings, comments and decision history. Agent 37 can analyse text only when the secure function and an AI key are configured. Without approved sources, BASE will not invent evidence.

Who can do what: creators submit and revise their own drafts; reviewers and workspace administrators can record approval decisions. The final approval is always a human action.

PRODUCT HANDBOOK

Feature guide

Learn what each area does, when to use it and which safeguards apply.

⌂01 · OVERVIEW

Your command center

See live review, source and membership counts, recent activity and setup tasks from the active workspace.

Detailed explanation

Every number is queried from the workspace database. Guest preview and new workspaces remain empty until real records are added. Setup tasks point to the next useful action rather than showing fabricated success metrics.

▤02 · REVIEW QUEUE

Source-aware content reviews

Submit a draft, run an AI-assisted check against approved sources, discuss findings and record a human decision.

Detailed explanation

Reviews store the original text, channel, submitter, status and structured findings. Agent 37 must cite a source from the active workspace or mark a concern as unverified. The AI never approves or publishes content itself. Text is the supported input for this version.

◈03 · SOURCE LIBRARY

Approved brand knowledge

Centralise policies, facts, tone guidance and terminology that reviewers can rely on.

Detailed explanation

Each source has an owner, type, body and optional review date. Only brand owners and admins can manage sources. The analysis function retrieves only sources from the active workspace and validates source references before returning findings.

✳04 · AGENT 37

Your website operations assistant

Ask questions, navigate pages, prepare actions and execute supported changes only after your confirmation.

Detailed explanation

Agent 37 is powered by a server-side model connection. It may propose changing your display name, creating a review or source, inviting a member, changing a review status or navigating to an app page. Every data-changing action is shown as a proposal and waits for your confirmation. It cannot perform arbitrary actions outside the documented website tools.

⌁05 · INSIGHTS

Operational patterns

Understand repeated findings, severity and completed reviews using saved workspace data.

Detailed explanation

Insights are calculated from records stored in the database. Empty states are intentional: BASE never substitutes sample analytics for actual results. These signals help improve guidance but are not a compliance certification.

♧06 · TEAM & ROLES

Clear responsibility

Understand access roles and see the people who can participate in the workspace.

Detailed explanation

Workspace membership is protected by database row-level security. Invitations use Supabase Auth email delivery and are only available to admins after the Vercel API, service-role environment variable and SMTP settings are configured.

◷07 · AUDIT HISTORY

Traceable decisions

Follow submissions, comments, source updates and reviewer actions in one timeline.

Detailed explanation

Audit events are separate records with actor, action, details and timestamp. This starter uses client-insert-only policies; add trusted server/database triggers before treating it as a tamper-proof compliance ledger.

⚙08 · SETTINGS

Personal controls

Manage your display name, appearance, exports and sign-in session.

Detailed explanation

Your display name is stored in the profile table and auth metadata. Theme and reduced-motion preferences are local to this browser. Workspace export includes only records your session is allowed to read.

⌕09 · FAST NAVIGATION

Jump to any workspace area

Use the Agent 37 shortcut or Ctrl/⌘ + K to open the assistant without hunting through menus.

Detailed explanation

From the assistant, ask to open the review queue, source library, insights, team, history, settings or feature guide. Navigation is immediate and does not change saved data. On mobile, the large floating Agent 37 button stays easy to reach.

◉10 · HUMAN APPROVAL

Safe AI-assisted actions

Review every proposed change before it is applied to your account or workspace.

Detailed explanation

Agent 37 separates suggestions from execution. A proposal describes the exact operation and displays Cancel and Confirm controls. Until you confirm, no data is changed. Permissions are checked again by the server, so a button cannot bypass your workspace role.

◫11 · SOURCE LIFECYCLE

Keep approved knowledge current

Organise source material by type and owner, and track when it should be reviewed again.

Detailed explanation

Use clear source names and choose a type such as brand guideline, approved fact, terminology or policy. Add a review due date where relevant. Reviewers can use the library as the evidence boundary for content checks. Only authorised brand owners and admins should maintain approved material. Update the source when the organisation approves a change; older review findings remain historical records.

▣12 · MULTI-WORKSPACE

Separate teams and projects

Keep different organisations or projects in distinct workspaces and switch between them.

Detailed explanation

Each workspace has its own members, role assignments, review queue, source library and activity history. Switching changes the active data context; it does not merge records. Create another workspace when you need a separate permission boundary. Access is enforced by database policies as well as the interface.

⇩13 · DATA EXPORT

Take a copy of workspace records

Export the records your signed-in account is permitted to read for reporting or backup workflows.

Detailed explanation

Exports are based on the active workspace and your current database permissions. Review the file before sharing it outside your organisation because it may contain draft content, comments or internal source material. Guest preview cannot export private workspace data because it has no authenticated workspace access.
